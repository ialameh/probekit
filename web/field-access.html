    Privacy and scope

    Analysis runs locally in this tab. Pasted results and selected files are not uploaded, stored in browser storage, or included in the page URL. Reloading or clearing the page discards the investigation. Downloaded reports remain on your device. Standard hosting request logs may record page visits; they do not receive your pasted results.

    Profile and directly assigned permission-set rows are compared by ID. Permission-set-group calculations and muting, assignment expiry, session activation, object and record permissions, licences, and screen visibility need separate checks.
